# Stainless Steel Fabrication Company Website

This context defines the language for a public company profile website for a stainless steel fabrication company. The website presents the company, its products and services, customer proof, and ways to start a project conversation.

## Offerings

**Dwita Mekatama**:
The company represented by this website, established in 2010, based in Bekasi, and publicly presented as PT. Dwita Mekatama.
_Avoid_: Dwika Mekatama, Dwita Mekar Tama

**Project owner**:
The primary website audience: a person or organization responsible for a project and looking for stainless steel products made or adapted to project requirements.
_Avoid_: General audience as the primary persona, end-user when the project context is meant

**Standard product**:
A product offering that can be presented as a recognizable catalog item, while still allowing project-specific adjustments.
_Avoid_: Ready stock, off-the-shelf product

**Custom fabrication**:
The primary offering in which a stainless steel product is made or adapted from a customer's requirements, dimensions, drawings, or project conditions.
_Avoid_: Bespoke manufacturing, custom order

**Product category**:
A broad group used to organize the company's stainless steel offerings for website visitors. A category may contain both standard examples and custom-fabricated work.
_Avoid_: Product type when referring to the website grouping

**General fabrication**:
The broader public-facing capability represented by the current company profile, covering fabricated equipment and components made to project requirements rather than only a fixed stainless steel catalog.
_Avoid_: Stainless-only fabrication when the service scope includes machinery, spare parts, or maintenance

The website's positioning is general fabrication with stainless steel as the primary strength and recognizable differentiator.

**Industrial equipment examples**:
The current product examples presented by the company are chain conveyor, modification tank, roller conveyor, and shoe barrier step bench.
_Avoid_: Treating these examples as an exhaustive product catalog

The v1 Products page groups offerings into **Fabrikasi Stainless Steel**, **Industrial Equipment**, and **Spare Parts**.

**Maintenance service**:
Service for periodic maintenance and troubleshooting intended to reduce machine downtime, alongside mechanical and electrical engineering support.
_Avoid_: After-sales support when a maintenance service is meant

Maintenance, troubleshooting, mechanical engineering, electrical engineering, and machine design or fabrication according to customer requirements are presented as supporting services rather than product categories, subject to current availability.

## Website language

**Company profile website**:
A public informational website that builds confidence in the company and guides prospective customers toward making contact.
_Avoid_: Online store, e-commerce site

**Customer logo**:
A visual mark representing an organization that has worked with the company, used as credibility evidence rather than as a product or service offering.
_Avoid_: Partner logo unless the relationship is specifically a partnership

**Static website**:
The v1 website scope in which public content is maintained as part of the site itself and there is no customer-facing content management system.
_Avoid_: Dynamic website when only a CMS is meant

**Primary language**:
Bahasa Indonesia for the first release; additional languages are out of scope until explicitly decided.
_Avoid_: Bilingual by default

**Products overview**:
The single v1 Products page that introduces product categories and examples without creating a separate detail page for every product. The page is a product-focused entry point; supporting services must not create a second product navigation system.
_Avoid_: Full catalog, product detail system

**Kontak**:
The Indonesian navigation label and page for starting a project conversation through WhatsApp or email.
_Avoid_: Contacts as the visible navigation label

**Legacy project photo**:
A photo from the existing company website that may be reused as a temporary visual asset for v1 until newer, higher-resolution photography is supplied.
_Avoid_: Stock photo, original project documentation unless provenance is confirmed

**Service area**:
The company's operational claim that it is based in Bekasi and serves project needs in Jabodetabek and surrounding areas.
_Avoid_: Nationwide service unless explicitly confirmed

**Active catalog**:
The existing product catalog document that remains valid and may be linked from the v1 Products page after its file location is confirmed.
_Avoid_: Archived catalog, unverified catalog

**Project inquiry**:
A request from a project owner about a stainless steel product, industrial equipment, spare part, or related fabrication or engineering need.
_Avoid_: Generic contact, online order

**Hero message**:
The primary Home-page promise: “Fabrikasi Stainless Steel untuk Kebutuhan Proyek Anda,” supported by the company's broader general-fabrication capability and experience since 2010.
_Avoid_: 100% quality as an unsupported promise
