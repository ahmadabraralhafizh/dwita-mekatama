# Company Profile Website PT. Dwita Mekatama

Label: ready-for-agent

## Problem Statement

The current web application is still the generic starter scaffold and does not represent PT. Dwita Mekatama as a credible company profile. A project owner looking for stainless steel products or general fabrication cannot quickly understand the company's capabilities, see examples of its work, recognize customer proof, or start a project inquiry through a clear contact path.

The existing company website contains useful business information, but its presentation is dated, its English copy is not aligned with the first-release language, and its product, service, and trust information is not organized around the needs of a project owner.

## Solution

Replace the starter experience with a responsive, Indonesian-language, static company profile website for PT. Dwita Mekatama. The site will be a full visual redesign with an industrial-premium direction: charcoal/graphite surfaces, stainless-steel silver accents, strong typography, generous spacing, and real company imagery.

The website will have five separate public pages with a shared navigation and visual shell:

- Home: the main promise, capabilities, featured product examples, proof, and project-inquiry CTA.
- About: company story, history since 2010, Bekasi base, general fabrication capability, supporting services, and company values.
- Products: one overview page grouped into Fabrikasi Stainless Steel, Industrial Equipment, and Spare Parts.
- Customer: a visual grid of approved customer logos, without case studies in v1.
- Kontak: one primary WhatsApp contact, one primary email contact, Bekasi location/service-area information, and the project-inquiry CTA.

The site's primary conversion is a project inquiry through WhatsApp, with email as the secondary contact. Contact values may be dummy values during implementation and must be replaced before production launch.

## Implementation Decisions

- Build the experience as a static React website within the existing TanStack Router and Tailwind-based application.
- Use five localized public routes: `/`, `/tentang`, `/produk`, `/pelanggan`, and `/kontak`.
- Replace the generic starter Home page and the single-link starter header with a shared company-profile shell containing brand identity, localized navigation, responsive behavior, and a consistent project-inquiry CTA.
- Use Indonesian content rewritten from the official company information rather than literal English translation.
- Use “PT. Dwita Mekatama” as the formal brand name and “Dwita Mekatama” where shorter copy is appropriate.
- Position the company as general fabrication with stainless steel as the primary strength and differentiator.
- Keep the Products page product-focused. Group it into Fabrikasi Stainless Steel, Industrial Equipment, and Spare Parts.
- Present Chain Conveyor, Modification Tank, Roller Conveyor, and Shoe Barrier Step Bench as product examples from the existing company profile.
- Present maintenance, troubleshooting, mechanical engineering, electrical engineering, and machine design/fabrication according to customer requirements as supporting capabilities, not additional navigation categories.
- Keep all product content on one overview page in v1; do not create individual product detail pages.
- Preserve the active catalog download affordance, using the existing catalog source during implementation once its document location is available.
- Use the existing customer-logo material with the approved reuse decision. Do not invent customer names, testimonials, project metrics, or case studies.
- Use existing company/project photography as temporary imagery. The design must tolerate replacement with newer photography later.
- Use a charcoal/graphite and stainless-silver visual palette, strong industrial typography, restrained borders, and high-impact project imagery. The result should be a deliberate industrial-premium redesign rather than a generic dashboard or template.
- Use a clear hero promise based on “Fabrikasi Stainless Steel untuk Kebutuhan Proyek Anda,” with general-fabrication context and the company's experience as supporting content.
- Use “Konsultasikan Proyek Anda” as the primary CTA throughout the site.
- Use one dummy WhatsApp value and one dummy email value during implementation. Keep the contact data centralized and make the placeholders easy to replace before launch.
- Show Bekasi as the company base and Jabodetabek serta sekitarnya as the service-area claim.
- Include the company story beginning in 2010 and the transition to PT. Dwita Mekatama in November 2013, based on the existing company profile.
- Use factual, softened claims such as “mengutamakan kualitas pengerjaan dan respons yang cepat”; do not use “100% quality” or other absolute claims without evidence.
- Do not add a CMS, database, authentication, API-backed inquiry workflow, or e-commerce behavior.
- Treat the site as a coherent public marketing surface. Component-level implementation details remain subordinate to route-level behavior and visual quality.

## Testing Decisions

- Tests and verification should focus on externally observable behavior, not component internals, CSS class choices, or exact DOM structure.
- Use the highest available seam: the public site surface across the five routes.
- The repository has no existing test harness or prior browser-test suite, so the initial verification should use a browser smoke-test checklist plus the existing production build and type-check commands.
- Verify that each route loads directly and through header navigation.
- Verify that the active navigation state and page headings identify the current page.
- Verify that the Home page exposes the hero message, core capabilities, product examples, customer proof, and primary CTA.
- Verify that the About page exposes the 2010 history, Bekasi base, general fabrication positioning, and supporting capabilities.
- Verify that the Products page exposes all three product groups, all four product examples, the custom-fabrication context, and the catalog download affordance.
- Verify that the Customer page displays the approved logo grid without broken images or misleading labels.
- Verify that the Kontak page exposes exactly one primary WhatsApp and one primary email placeholder, with correct link behavior.
- Verify that all repeated project-inquiry CTAs resolve to the same centralized contact values.
- Verify that the site remains usable at mobile and desktop viewport widths, including navigation, image cropping, card layout, and CTA placement.
- Verify keyboard focus visibility, semantic headings, image alternative text, link names, and sufficient color contrast for the principal reading and conversion paths.
- Run the existing production build and type-check command after implementation.
- Treat a broken route, missing core content, broken CTA/catalog link, overflow on mobile, or unreadable contrast as a failure of the public site surface.

## Out of Scope

- CMS or admin editing for products, customer logos, catalog, or contact information.
- E-commerce, online ordering, pricing tables, quotation calculators, payment, or checkout.
- User accounts, authentication, dashboards, or customer portals.
- Backend form submission, lead storage, CRM integration, email service integration, or WhatsApp automation.
- Individual product detail pages or a searchable/filterable product catalog.
- Customer case studies, testimonials, project metrics, or customer names beyond approved logo presentation.
- A bilingual or English-language version.
- New professional photography; legacy project photos are sufficient for v1.
- Inventing material grades, finishing options, production capacity, project counts, certifications, or other unsupported business claims.
- Final production contact details during the initial dummy-data implementation.
- A separate services navigation item; supporting capabilities remain within the agreed page structure.
